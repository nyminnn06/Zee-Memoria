Hannah Yeoh is the current Minister in the Prime Minister’s Department (Federal Territories). Yeoh previously served as the Minister of Youth and Sports from 2022 to 2025 and earlier as the Deputy Minister of Women, Family and Community Development, a role she held from mid-2018 to early 2020. At present, she is serving her second term as the Member of Parliament for P117 Segambut, a parliamentary constituency in the Federal Territory of Kuala Lumpur, which she first won in 2018 during the historic general election which saw the Pakatan Harapan coalition come to power. Prior to that, Yeoh was the two-term State Assemblyman for the constituency of N31 Subang Jaya (2008 – 2018). During her tenure as State Assemblyman, Yeoh made history by becoming the first woman to be elected as the Speaker of the Selangor State Legislative Assembly in 2013 and the youngest of any legislative body in Malaysia.
